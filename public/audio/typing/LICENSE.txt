Seoul Keys modifications: extract three individual strikes, convert to mono
44.1 kHz PCM, remove low-frequency rumble, normalize modestly, and fade the
edges. key-wide is the recorded wide-key/Enter strike, reused for Space.
The completion bell and carriage-return sound are not changed.
